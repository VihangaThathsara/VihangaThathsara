## Typeface outlines

Space Grotesk and Manrope are used for the portfolio typography and the repository-generated statistics cards. Font glyph outlines are retained in `scripts/font-paths.json` so the cards render consistently.

- Space Grotesk: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk
- Manrope: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

Both typefaces are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their complete notices are in `licenses/SpaceGrotesk-OFL.txt` and `licenses/Manrope-OFL.txt`.
